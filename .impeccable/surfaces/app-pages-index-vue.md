# lixsos.com marketing site

Scope: every public route (home, services, service detail, work, case study, ERP, about, contact, error). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: SME owners in Egypt and the Gulf choosing one partner for software, marketing and ERP. Action: start a project / book a call / request an ERP demo. Proof: the shipped project screenshots. Stats and testimonials stay hidden (placeholders).

## Direction contract

THESIS: LIXSOS printed like currency people trust. Each service is a denomination with its own ink; Arabic and English are the two faces of one note. Refuses the dark-navy neon-gradient agency hero with glass cards and particle canvases.

OWN-WORLD: Light green-grey security paper ground (dark theme: deep green-black "night note"), intaglio ink near-black text, one green intaglio primary, serial red for numerals and markers. Hairline guilloche rosettes and wave bands drawn in SVG; square-cornered panels framed by fine double rules; Bodoni-style engraved Latin display, Amiri Naskh for Arabic display, Public Sans / Noto Sans Arabic text, mono only for serials. No gradients, glows, glass or blur.

STORY: Visitor sees what LIXSOS makes (software, marketing, ERP) on the face of one note, believes it from the uncut sheet of real shipped projects, and acts via Start a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width note framed by a guilloche band. Headline and intro at start side, live guilloche rosette with the logo watermark at end side, four denomination tabs along the bottom edge (Web, Mobile, Marketing, ERP: mobile apps are a separate service in the catalogue, so the count follows the real offer), primary "Start a project" plus "See our work" inside the note. A numbered serial printed twice (top-end, bottom-start) with bilingual microtext between guilloche bands at the top and bottom edges. On phones the rosette shrinks so the denominations stay close to the fold.

FORM: Banknote Engraving, candidate 6 of 7 (assigned). seed:c9232566e50c4092. Raises: colour reserved for the active denomination (glazier); work packed edge to edge as an uncut sheet (catalogue); process as fixed numbered stations (darkroom); state as line form (rail). Signature interaction: hovering a denomination prints its ring of the rosette at full ink while others fade; switching language flips the note to its other face. Motion: rosette strokes draw in once on load, like a print pass; no scroll reveals, no smooth-scroll, no rotating words. At rest the rosette prints in one green ink; a denomination's own ink appears only while it is hovered or focused.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
